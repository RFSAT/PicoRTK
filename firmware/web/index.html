PicoRTK · RTK GNSS rover

Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W with a Quectel LG290P quad-band receiver (L1, L2, L5, E6), for centimetre-level RTK. RTCM comes over Wi-Fi from up to four NTRIP sources in priority order (own base, VRS, nearest station, public caster), with automatic failover and every frame checked. Without internet: Galileo HAS, about 20 cm.

History

One record every 10 s (method, corrections live or stale with their age and base, estimate, actual error, position, speed, satellites, HDOP), kept in the Pico's flash across restarts and power cuts. It is written to flash every 5 minutes and when you shut down or restart from this page, so pulling the power loses at most the last 5 minutes. When full, the oldest records go first.

Failover

Sources are tried in order. A failure (no connection, no reply, rejected login or mount point, no RTCM for the timeout, or a correction age above the limit) moves to the next source. After every source has failed in a row the device waits (back-off) before starting again from source 1.

0 turns off "Return to source 1" and "Max correction age". The bench position is sent to VRS and nearest-station casters while the receiver has no fix, so they start streaming; leave latitude and longitude at 0 to turn it off.
